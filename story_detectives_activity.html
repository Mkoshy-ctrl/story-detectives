Story Detectives: Find the Clues!

Read the extract carefully. Work as a team to uncover the story elements and investigate the clue that creates suspense.

🗂️ Activity 1: Read the extract

Step 1 Label the five story elements in the text. Select the best label for each element below.

Detective tip: To circle descriptive language, click a word or phrase in the extract. Click it again to remove the circle.

🕵️ Step 2: Investigate the suspense clue

Identify the clue that creates suspense. Explain how it makes the reader want to know what happens next.

Think carefully. Use evidence from the text to support your ideas.
